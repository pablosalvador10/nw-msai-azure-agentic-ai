# Weather pauses and closures

## Weather pause

We pause new rentals across the system when lightning is detected within 10 miles, or when sustained winds exceed 40 mph. The app shows a banner while a pause is active.

## Rides in progress during a pause

If you are riding when a pause begins, dock as soon as it is safe. You are not charged for any minutes after the pause began.

## Planned closures

Stations can close for street events or maintenance. Closures are announced in the app at least 48 hours in advance.
