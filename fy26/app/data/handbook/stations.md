# Stations and docking

## Opening hours

Stations are open 24 hours a day, every day. The four lakefront stations, LS-01 to LS-04, are seasonal: they close from December 1 to March 31.

## When a station is full

If the station where you want to end your ride has no free docks, tap "Station full" in the app. You get 15 extra minutes free to reach another station. This is the dock-full grace period.

## Broken docks

If a dock will not lock your bike, try another dock at the same station. If none works, use the "Station full" button and report the broken dock in the app.

## Live availability

The app shows, for every station, the number of classic bikes, e-bikes and free docks. Availability updates every minute.
