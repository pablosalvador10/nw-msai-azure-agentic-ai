# Damage and lost bikes

## Report damage before you ride

Inspect the bike before you unlock it. If you report damage in the app before or within 10 minutes of unlocking, you are never charged for that damage.

## Damage fees

If a rider damages a bike, we charge the repair cost, up to $300 per incident.

## Lost or stolen bikes

A bike not docked within 24 hours of unlock is considered lost.

- Lost classic bike fee: $1,200.
- Lost e-bike fee: $2,500.

If the bike was stolen and you file a police report within 48 hours of unlock, the fee drops to $150. Send the report number to Support.

Lost-bike and damage fees are not covered by the $50 fare cap.
