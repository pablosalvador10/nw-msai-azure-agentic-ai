# E-bikes

## Battery

- A full battery gives about 40 miles of assisted riding.
- The display warns you when the battery drops to 15%.
- Bikes with less than 20% battery cannot be unlocked.

## If the battery dies during your ride

Dock the bike at the nearest station and contact Support within 24 hours. We refund the e-bike per-minute charges for that ride as ride credit. Unlock fees are not refunded.

## Charging

Riders never charge e-bikes. Our operations team swaps batteries at stations overnight.
