# Ride rules

## Who can ride

- Riders must be at least 16 years old.
- Riders under 18 must wear a helmet. Helmets are recommended for everyone.
- One rider per bike. No passengers, including children in carriers.

## Speed

- E-bikes provide pedal assist up to 20 mph.
- On the Lakefront Trail, every bike, classic or e-bike, must stay under 10 mph.

## Ride length

- A single ride can last up to 24 hours.
- A bike that has not been docked 24 hours after unlock is treated as lost. See "Damage and lost bikes".

## Ending a ride

A ride ends only when the bike is docked and the dock light turns green. Leaving a bike next to a station does not end the ride.
