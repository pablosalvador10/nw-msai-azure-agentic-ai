# Refunds and ride credits

## Refunds

- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
- A membership or Student Pass can be refunded in full within 7 days of purchase if no rides were taken.
- A Day Pass cannot be refunded after its first ride.
- Billing disputes must be raised within 60 days of the charge.

## Ride credits

- Ride credits are applied automatically to your next rides.
- Ride credits never expire and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Who can approve what

A Support agent can issue up to $25 in ride credit per case. Anything above $25 requires approval from a Support supervisor, so the case is escalated.
