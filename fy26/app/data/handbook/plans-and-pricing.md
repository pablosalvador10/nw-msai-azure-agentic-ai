# Plans and pricing

Lakeshore Ride offers four ways to ride. Prices are in US dollars and include tax.

## Single ride (pay as you go)

- Unlock fee: $1.25 per ride.
- Classic bike: $0.20 per minute.
- E-bike: $0.40 per minute.
- No minutes are included.

## Day Pass

- $16, valid for 24 hours from the moment of purchase.
- Classic bike: the first 45 minutes of every ride are included. After 45 minutes, $0.20 per minute.
- E-bike: $0.25 per minute from the first minute.
- No unlock fee.

## Membership

- $20 per month, or $150 per year.
- Classic bike: the first 45 minutes of every ride are included. After 45 minutes, $0.15 per minute.
- E-bike: $0.18 per minute from the first minute.
- No unlock fee.

## Student Pass

- $55 per academic quarter. Requires a valid .edu email address, re-verified every quarter.
- Classic bike: the first 60 minutes of every ride are included. After 60 minutes, $0.15 per minute.
- E-bike: $0.15 per minute from the first minute.
- No unlock fee.

## Fare cap

No single ride costs more than $50 in ride fees, whatever the plan. The cap does not apply to damage or lost-bike fees.

## How minutes are counted

A ride starts when the bike is unlocked and ends when it is docked. Partial minutes are rounded up to the next whole minute.
