# Safety and contacting Support

## If you are hurt

Call 911 first. Then report the incident in the app within 24 hours. Your ride is ended for you and charges stop at the time of the report.

## Response times

- Safety reports: answered within 1 hour, day or night.
- Billing questions: answered within 2 business days.
- Everything else: answered within 3 business days.

## How to reach Support

- In-app chat: 24 hours a day.
- Phone: (555) 010-0100, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day.
