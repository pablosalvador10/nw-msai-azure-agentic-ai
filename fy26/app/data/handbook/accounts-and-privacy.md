# Accounts and privacy

## Your account

- To change your email address, verify the new address from the app.
- Account deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

## What we keep

- Ride history is kept for 2 years, then deleted.
- We never share a rider's phone number, email or ride history with other riders.

## What Support will never ask for

Support will never ask for your password or your full card number. If someone does, it is not us.
