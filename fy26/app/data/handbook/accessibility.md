# Adaptive bikes and accessibility

## Adaptive Ride program

Hand cycles, tricycles and recumbent bikes are available at three stations: LS-07, LS-12 and LS-18.

- Reserve an adaptive bike through Support at least 24 hours in advance.
- The first 60 minutes of every adaptive ride are free. After that, standard classic rates for your plan apply.
- A companion can ride a standard bike next to you at no extra charge for the same duration.

## Accessible app

The app supports screen readers and large text. Support can also start and end rides for you by phone.
